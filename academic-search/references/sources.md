# Academic Sources & Search Tips

## Major Databases
- **Google Scholar**: Best for broad searches across all disciplines.
- **arXiv.org**: Primary source for Physics, Mathematics, Computer Science, Quantitative Biology, Quantitative Finance, Statistics, Electrical Engineering and Systems Science, and Economics.
- **PubMed**: Life sciences and biomedical topics.
- **IEEE Xplore**: Electrical engineering, computer science, and electronics.
- **Scopus/Web of Science**: Comprehensive curated citation databases (usually requires institutional access).

## Search Operators
- `author:"John Doe"`: Search for specific authors.
- `intitle:"Deep Learning"`: Search for keywords in the title.
- `source:"Nature"`: Search in specific journals.
- `year:2023-2024`: Filter by date range.

## Citation Formats
### APA 7th Edition
Author, A. A., & Author, B. B. (Year). Title of the article. *Title of Periodical*, *Volume*(Issue), pages. https://doi.org/xx.xxx/yyyy

### BibTeX
```bibtex
@article{key,
  author = {Author, A. and Author, B.},
  title = {Title of the article},
  journal = {Journal Name},
  year = {Year},
  volume = {Volume},
  number = {Issue},
  pages = {Pages},
  doi = {DOI}
}
```
