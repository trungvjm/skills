# VJST Reject Email

## Goal

Write a short English rejection email for VJST manuscripts. Keep the decision firm, the tone professional, and the reasons academic.

## Inputs To Use

Use the manuscript title, submission ID, author name, screening report, ARS review, reference audit, or user-provided notes if available. Do not invent facts, reviewer comments, plagiarism findings, DOI errors, or policy grounds.

If the evidence is thin, write a narrower email rather than adding generic reasons.

## Required Style

- Keep the email concise: subject, salutation, thanks, decision, 3-5 short reason paragraphs, close.
- Focus on academic reasons: unclear contribution, unsupported claims, weak validation, insufficient quantitative evidence, flawed assumptions, methodological gaps, reproducibility limits, or unreliable references.
- Use direct language: "we are unable to proceed with it" and "we cannot accept the manuscript".
- Do not say "cannot be sent for external peer review" unless the user asks for that wording.
- Do not invite resubmission or discuss "future submission" unless the user asks for it.
- Do not over-explain formatting issues. Mention reference or metadata problems only when they affect academic reliability.
- Default to English. Use Vietnamese only if the user asks.

## Avoid AI Writing

Apply the avoid-ai-writing principles even if the skill is not explicitly invoked:

- Avoid template phrases such as "the decision is based on the following academic concerns", "in its present form", "scholarly reliability", and "academic concerns" when plainer wording works.
- Avoid numbered transitions like "First, Second, Third, Fourth" in the email body.
- Avoid inflated words such as "robust", "pivotal", "comprehensive", "significant" unless they come from the evidence and are necessary.
- Avoid em dashes, title-case section headings, emoji, bold emphasis, and bullet-heavy formatting in the email.
- Prefer plain verbs: use, show, state, report, support, correct, accept, proceed.

## Email Pattern

```text
Subject: Decision on manuscript [ID]

Dear Dr. [Name],

Thank you for submitting your manuscript, "[Title]", to the Vietnam Journal of Science and Technology.

After reviewing the manuscript, we regret to inform you that we are unable to proceed with it.

[Reason paragraph 1: core academic defect, usually novelty/contribution.]

[Reason paragraph 2: evidence does not support claims, if applicable.]

[Reason paragraph 3: validation/methodology/reproducibility weakness.]

[Optional reason paragraph 4: assumptions/reference audit if academically relevant.]

For these reasons, we cannot accept the manuscript.

Thank you for considering the Vietnam Journal of Science and Technology.

Sincerely,

Section Editor
Vietnam Journal of Science and Technology
```

## Final Check

Before returning the email, reread it once and remove:

- resubmission invitations unless requested;
- "external peer review" phrasing unless requested;
- generic filler;
- AI-sounding transitions;
- claims not present in the source notes.
